# Regulatory and governance boundary

Official material checked on 2026-10-17: [EUR-Lex consolidated Regulation 2023/1115, 26 December 2025](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02023R1115-20251226), [Commission implementation resources](https://green-forum.ec.europa.eu/nature-and-biodiversity/deforestation-regulation-implementation_en), and [official Information System documentation](https://green-forum.ec.europa.eu/nature-and-biodiversity/deforestation-regulation-implementation/information-system-deforestation-regulation_en). The consolidated page lists amendments 2024/3234 and 2025/2650; the Commission page also points to later product-scope and system materials. Authentic Official Journal acts govern; a consolidated text is a documentation aid. This bounded upgrade does not validate all later legal changes or change regulatory rules.

Legal context: EUDR due diligence covers information, risk assessment/mitigation and product-specific traceability, including production geolocation. Supplier names/addresses alone do not establish origin or compliance. The official Information System distinguishes legally effective production submissions from non-legal-value acceptance testing.

Internal governance: human identity approval, evidence preservation, alias verification, audit retention and production approval gates are KODA control choices. Technical design: alias/queue/merge tables and canonical redirects implement those controls; these particular table designs are not asserted to be statutory EUDR requirements. FSC certification is not an automatic EUDR approval. Identity matching/merging does not approve legality, deforestation-free assessment, GEO, evidence or DDS. 43_REGULATION_RULES remains unchanged; any rule update requires a separate authorized legal review.
